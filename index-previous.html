About us

A hospital built by and for the community.

CRT Hospital is a charitable trust hospital in Pappammalpuram, near T.V. Renganathapuram. Set among coconut groves, it gives families from the surrounding villages a trusted place for care without travelling far.

Compassion

Every patient is treated like family — with patience, respect and time to listen.

Close to home

Care within reach of the villages we serve, so no one has to delay a visit.

Community first

As a charitable trust, our purpose is service to the people of this area.

Open to everyone

Step-free access, accessible restroom and seating, and free parking.

Our people
Care we offer

Everyday care, done with attention.

From a routine check-up to guidance on your next step, our team is here to help. Please ask at the front desk about services available on the day of your visit.

01

General consultation

See a doctor for fever, cough, pain, infections and everyday health concerns.

02

First aid

Prompt attention for minor injuries, cuts, burns and wounds that need dressing.

03

Women & child health

Check-ups and advice for mothers, infants and growing children.

04

Care for elders

Regular follow-up for blood pressure, sugar and other long-term conditions.

05

Health awareness

Guidance on nutrition, hygiene and preventive care for the whole family.

06

Referral support

When specialist care is needed, we help you understand where to go next.
